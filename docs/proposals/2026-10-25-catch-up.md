## 1. Summary

- The CLI is less outdated than assumed. Docs match the parser (one bad README example), all 421 tests pass, and `audit`, `--custom-fields` (#32) and `--outcome` are shipped and documented.
- Two things break today: `deps create` omits the required `lag_days`, and `activities bulk-update` very likely sends a payload shape the server rejects.
- Three open issues (#33, #34, #35) are release-gated: nothing on preprod `95addac48` has retired `content_contains` or added the 128 MiB cap yet, but v1.1.24 / v1.1.42 will. They must ship before those releases.
- The team does not shell out to `pcxa`. It imports `pcxa._api.APIClient` in-process and patches custom fields by label itself, so the CLI's validation and error messages never reach its jobs. The gaps are label-based custom fields and the unclear edge-block error.
- The backend has about 40 routes the CLI cannot reach (archive/restore/reparent, CSV and P6 import, analytics, baselines, calendars). These are P3 candidates.
